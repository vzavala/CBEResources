    Quick start — a distillation column you can run, break and inspect

    1. Start the plant. Press the red ▶ button at top left. The clock shows plant time; change the speed-up to run faster. ⏩ +6 h jumps straight to steady state.
    2. Operate it. In Operate, move the reboiler duty, reflux ratio, feed flow or feed strength. Each change is marked on the trends.
    3. Control it. In Control, switch the temperature and level loops between Auto (the controller holds the setpoint) and Manual (you set the output), tune the gains and change setpoints. Loop trends plots each loop.
    4. Watch the response. The chart at right shows product strength. Dynamic Trends opens all six charts; Stage Profiles and McCabe–Thiele show the column tray by tray.
    5. Look inside. Click any label in the 3D view (a tray, the condenser, the reboiler) for a close-up and its numbers. Double-click to fly there.
    6. Cause trouble. In Faults, trip the heater, cut the cooling water, add foaming or damage a tray, then watch the alarms.
    7. Check the model. Model vs. Data compares the twin with runs measured on our lab column. Math shows every equation with live values.
