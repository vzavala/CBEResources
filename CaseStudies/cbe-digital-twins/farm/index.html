      User guide

      A live model of a Wisconsin dairy and crop farm. Grow a season and see where fertilizer, water and phosphorus go.

      Disclaimer: This is for educational purposes only. This digital twin is based on a combination of public data, scientific publications and fictionalized settings.

      How to explore

      • Overview: the whole farm; the Farm camera button returns here.
      • Numbered red spots (or the ChemE spots list, left): zoom to a part and open its card.
      • Drag to orbit · scroll / pinch to zoom · right-drag to pan.
      • Camera buttons (bottom): Farm, Corn field, Tractor, Barn & digester, Grain bins, Stream & lake, Soil & roots, Plant cell, Molecules.
      • Play / Pause, Speed and the day slider: move through April–October.

      Buttons and views

      • Real world: why farms matter (food, energy, water).
      • Conditions: live labels in 3D and the control panels.
      • Subject chips (Balances, Thermo, …): the chemical engineering behind each part.
      • ∑ Model: equations, parameters, assumptions, validation.
      • Links to Courses: which UW–Madison CBE courses use this twin, with problem sets.
      • ⤢ on the side panel: pop it out as a floating window; ⤡ docks it.

      Drone crop map

      • What it measures: the drone's camera records red and near-infrared light. Healthy leaves absorb red and reflect near-infrared.
      • Colors: green = dense, healthy crop · yellow = thin or stressed · brown = bare soil.
      • How the farmer uses it: zones that look weak get checked; the Variable rate controller sets fertilizer zone by zone.

      Change conditions, watch the results

      • Weather, water and seed: wet / normal / dry year, irrigation, cover crop, seed trait → yield and water use.
      • Nitrogen fertilizer: rate, timing, uniform vs variable → yield, nitrate in drainage, losses to air.
      • Autonomous sprayer: switch to Remote control and drive it with the arrows.
      • Dairy: digester and nutrient recovery → electricity and the lake's algae.
      • Each panel ends with What this means in plain words.
