User guide

A live model of a pastillation line: a drop former drips hot melt onto a 16 m water-cooled steel belt. The physics runs in your browser, so every control changes the result.

How to explore
  • The page opens on an overview of the whole line.
  • Click a numbered spot, or its row in the list on the left, to zoom in and open its card.
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Camera buttons (bottom right): Overview, Drop former, Cooling belt, Under-belt sprays, Thermal camera, Discharge.
Buttons and views
  • Plant view: the line as it looks; pastilles turn from amber to cream as they cool.
  • Thermal view: belt and pastilles coloured by temperature, 72–212 °F.
  • Real world: what pastillation is used for.
  • Conditions: opens the controller and clog panels and shows the step counter.
  • Subject buttons (Balances … Design): a short card on where that subject appears.
  • Pause / Run, Step (one 0.5 s step), Rate (1×, 4×, 12× real time).
  • ⤢ on the side panel: pop it out as a floating window; ⤡ docks it.
Change conditions and watch
  • PID auto / Manual: let the controller set the belt speed, or set it yourself.
  • Setpoint and gains: the target temperature of the last row and how hard the controller pushes.
  • Inject clog, or click a nozzle bar: block nozzles for 10–60 s.
  • Clog profile: measured clog chances, no clogs, or a uniform 20 %.
  • Watch the trend chart, the What this means boxes and the thermal camera strip.
  • Show run data (CSV): every step of this run, to copy into a spreadsheet. Restart run: start again.
