    About this digital twin

    A dynamic tray-by-tray model of an ethanol–water sieve-tray column12, with two tray efficiencies fitted to four student lab runs17. Equations and checks: ∑ Model. An integrative case study for UW–Madison CBE students.

    It resembles the column used in the department's summer lab; it is a generic educational model, not a copy of that equipment, and not for operating or safety decisions.

    User guide

    A live model of a 14-tray column separating ethanol from water. It resembles the column in the department's summer lab and is fitted to four student runs.

    How to explore

    • 🏭 Opens on the Overview of the lab bay, running at steady state.
    • ⬇ After a few seconds the feed weakens by itself: watch the trends at the bottom respond.
    • 🔴 Click a numbered spot, or its row in the list, to zoom in.
    • 🖱 Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · Overview returns.
    • ▶ Pause / Play and Speed (default: 1 real second = 20 s of plant time).

    Buttons and views

    • Ethanol / Temperature: colour the liquid on each tray by ethanol content or by temperature.
    • Column · Tray · Feed · Reboiler · Condenser · Products · Console: close-up cameras.
    • McCabe–Thiele / Tray profile: the two tray-by-tray charts in the panel.
    • Real world: why distillation is chemical engineering.
    • Flow diagram: the same column as a process diagram, with live flows, compositions and heat duties.
    • Conditions: live status tag, and the operating and control panels.
    • Learn by subject: one stop per course subject.
    • ⤢ / ⤡: pop the right panel out as a window, or dock it.

    Change conditions

    • Operate the column: lab-run set-ups, reflux, steam, feed, cooling water, tray efficiency, faults.
    • Control loops: switch a temperature loop to Auto and retune it.
    • Skip 30 min jumps ahead; Repeat feed upset replays the standard disturbance.
    • Watch the trend charts, the distillate strength and the What this means boxes.
