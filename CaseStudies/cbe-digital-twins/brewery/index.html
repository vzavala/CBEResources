    User guide

    A live model of one 50 hL batch of beer, grain to keg. Change the recipe and the batch is recalculated.

    How to explore

    • 🏭 Opens on the Overview of the whole brewery.
    • 🔴 Click a numbered spot, or its row in the list, to zoom in.
    • 🖱 Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · Overview returns.
    • ▶ Play runs the batch (1 s = 6 min of brew day, or 5 h in the cellar); Speed changes it; drag the slider to any moment.

    Buttons and views

    • Closed tank / Cutaway: the first fermenter whole, or sliced open to show beer, foam and yeast.
    • Batch timeline: the stages as a coloured bar, brew day in hours then cellar in days.
    • Yeast: microscope view of the cells multiplying, then clumping.
    • Fermenter · Brewhouse · Kettle · Cooler · Cellar · Kegs: close-up cameras.
    • Real world: why a brewery is chemical engineering.
    • Conditions: live status tag and the recipe and cellar controls.
    • Learn by subject: one stop per course subject.
    • ⤢ / ⤡: pop the right panel out as a window, or dock it.

    Change conditions

    • Recipe & brew day: style, malt, mash temperature, hops, boil.
    • Fermentation & cellar: temperature, yeast, rest, carbonation, cooling on or off.
    • Watch the trend charts at the bottom, the ready day and the What this means boxes.
